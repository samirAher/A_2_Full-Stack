ABOUT US

KBTCOE

Karmaveer Adv. Baburao Ganpatrao Thakare College of Engineering, Nashik is committed to providing quality technical education, innovation, research and excellent placement opportunities.

College History

Established with the vision of creating highly skilled engineers, KBTCOE has become one of the leading engineering institutes in Nashik. The institute provides modern infrastructure, experienced faculty, advanced laboratories and industry-oriented education.

Vision

To become a center of excellence in engineering education, research and innovation while developing globally competent professionals with ethical values.

Mission

Provide quality education, encourage innovation, promote research, enhance entrepreneurship, and prepare students for successful careers.
